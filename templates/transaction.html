Why Unlimited Approvals are Dangerous

When users grant 2^256 - 1 allowance, the spender contract can transfer the user's tokens indefinitely at any time in the future.

If the spender is later compromised, possesses an upgradeable proxy backdoor, or was a phishing impersonator, user tokens are swept without requiring a new approval prompt.
